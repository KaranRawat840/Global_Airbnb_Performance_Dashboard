# LinkedIn Post Draft

I analyzed **280K Airbnb listings and 5.3M reviews across 10 global cities** in Power BI — here's what stood out. 🏠📊

A few numbers that surprised me:

📍 Paris alone makes up 23% of all listings in the dataset — nearly 2x New York, the next biggest market.

⭐ Superhosts consistently outperform: 97.0 avg rating vs 92.3 for everyone else. Being a Superhost isn't just a badge — it correlates with real, measurable quality.

💰 Price tracks size, not quality. Bedrooms and capacity correlate with price far more than review score does. A great rating doesn't buy you a premium — more space does.

📉 The clearest signal in the whole dataset: reviews grew every year from 2011 to a peak of 1.6M in 2019 — then COVID-19 cut 2020 volume by more than half, and 2021 barely registers. You can see the pandemic in the data without even looking at a date.

🔑 Only 41% of listings are instant-bookable. Even in 2021, most hosts still wanted to personally approve their guests.

Built entirely in Power BI with 32 custom DAX measures across 3 report pages (Overview, Ratings, Reviews). Full write-up, data model, and the .pbix file are on my GitHub — link in comments. 👇
